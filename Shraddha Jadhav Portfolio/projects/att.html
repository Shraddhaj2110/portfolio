Program Overview

Modernize the lifecycle platform without interrupting the customer journey.

Business need

AT&T serves a large mobile customer base with multiple recurring communications around plan usage, upgrades, device eligibility, service education and retention. The migration objective is to move active campaigns from Adobe Campaign Classic to Salesforce Marketing Cloud, simplify lifecycle orchestration and improve the ability to retarget customers based on recent engagement.

Campaign focus

Build a priority audience of mobile customers who engaged with AT&T communications or digital experiences within the last 30 days, then retarget them with relevant upgrade, add-a-line, device or plan offers while excluding customers with active service cases, recent purchases, opt-outs or conflicting offers.

Migration Architecture

Adobe Campaign Classic to Salesforce Marketing Cloud.

The migration is treated as a controlled product move: inventory first, map the data model, rebuild reusable components, validate one journey end to end, then move waves of programs with rollback and monitoring plans.
