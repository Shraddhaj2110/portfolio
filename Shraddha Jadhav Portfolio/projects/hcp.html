HCP Disease Education & Clinical Learning Program

A coordinated education program that helps priority healthcare professionals discover relevant disease-state content, continue learning through personalized resources, and move into expert-led education when their behavior shows deeper interest.

Campaign Objective

Increase disease awareness and make clinical education easier for HCPs to access and continue.

The campaign starts with an eligible HCP audience and uses engagement behavior to determine the next educational touchpoint. An HCP who opens but does not click receives a different follow-up from someone who downloads a resource, revisits the hub or registers for an event.

Audience

Priority HCPs segmented by specialty, market, consent, engagement history and education interests.

Primary Goal

Drive qualified visits to disease education, increase useful resource engagement and create a clear path to deeper learning.

Success Signals

Resource downloads, repeat content visits, webinar registration and attendance, and appropriate CRM follow-up signals.

Governance

Consent, suppression, approved content, market eligibility, frequency and field-routing rules are validated before activation.

HCP Journey

One education journey with multiple personalized paths.

01Qualify

Eligible HCP

Specialty, market, consent, contactability and suppression checks.

02Introduce

Disease education email

Personalized topic introduction with a single education CTA.

03Explore

Clinical resource hub

Modular content tailored by specialty, language and prior behavior.

04Reinforce

Paid activation

Google, Meta and LinkedIn reinforce relevant educational content.

05Deepen

Webinar / event

Invite, confirmation, reminders, attendance and post-event nurture.

06Continue

CRM context

High-intent signals and content history support appropriate follow-up.

Email Template

Personalized disease education email built from reusable content blocks.

The template is intentionally modular. The hero, headline, education card, event card, CTA and footer can change independently based on specialty, market, language, engagement history and campaign eligibility.
