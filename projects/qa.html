Campaign QA & Release Governance

A campaign-control framework covering metadata, data validation, rendering, approvals, launch readiness, exception handling and auditability.

Quality metrics use sample data.
Operational Controls

Make every send traceable before it becomes scalable.

Trigger rules

Validate when the campaign is allowed to enter production.

  • Status must be Approved
  • Start/end dates must be active
  • Audience and suppression rules must resolve
  • Required metadata must be complete
  • Consent and market rules must pass

Fallbacks

Protect continuity when an asset, locale or feed is unavailable.

  • Use approved default content
  • Fall back to global English where permitted
  • Retain last approved version for feed failures
  • Hold regulated sends for manual review
  • Log every fallback event

Auditability

Keep evidence of what changed, who approved it and what was delivered.

  • Content ID + version
  • Publish date/time + user
  • Campaign usage history
  • Approval and exception logs
  • Retention and compliance records
Campaign Checks

Pre-launch checks that protect the customer experience.

The QA checklist covers the campaign from audience selection through delivery and tracking. Each check has an owner, evidence, status and sign-off so launch decisions are consistent and traceable.

1. Basic setup & sanity

  • Campaign name, ID, brand, market and channel are correct
  • Start/end date, send time and timezone match the approved brief
  • Correct template, folder, program and delivery type selected
  • Seed/test profiles included and production list excluded during QA
  • Campaign status remains Draft/QA until sign-off is complete

2. Audience & data

  • Eligibility logic matches the business requirement
  • Deduplication key and contact identity are validated
  • Opt-outs, suppressions, control groups and exclusions applied
  • Counts reconciled against source data and expected ranges
  • Personalization fields have null/default handling

3. Content, brand & personalization

  • Subject line, preheader, sender name and reply-to are approved
  • Brand, legal, product and market copy match approved content
  • Dynamic blocks resolve correctly for every segment/locale
  • Images have alt text and approved renditions
  • Fallback content is tested for missing profile values

4. Links, tracking & analytics

  • Every CTA, image link and deep link opens the correct destination
  • UTM parameters and campaign IDs match the measurement plan
  • Redirects, app links and landing-page tracking are validated
  • Conversion events fire correctly in analytics/CRM
  • No staging, preview or internal URLs remain in production content

5. Rendering & accessibility

  • Email tested across major clients, devices and dark mode
  • SMS length, encoding, short links and STOP language checked
  • Push/in-app copy fits device limits without truncation
  • Text hierarchy, contrast, alt text and mobile responsiveness reviewed
  • Fonts, spacing, images and buttons render consistently

6. Compliance & permissions

  • Consent, preference-center and market privacy rules applied
  • Required disclosures, terms and unsubscribe mechanisms present
  • Regulatory/medical/legal approvals attached where required
  • Frequency caps and contact policies respected
  • Restricted audiences and sensitive attributes excluded

7. Deployment readiness

  • Final audience count and expected send volume approved
  • Dependencies, API feeds and scheduled jobs are healthy
  • Fallback and rollback paths are documented
  • Monitoring owner and launch communication channel confirmed
  • Final proof and sign-off recorded before activation

8. Post-send validation

  • Delivery, bounce and error rates checked after launch
  • Sample live records verified in CRM and campaign platform
  • Tracking events and conversions confirmed
  • Exceptions logged with severity, owner and resolution
  • Final QA evidence retained with campaign metadata
