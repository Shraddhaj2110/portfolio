Campaign Testing & Experimentation

A structured testing program for lifecycle campaigns covering hypothesis design, audience splits, creative variants, journey logic, quality checks, statistical readouts and rollout decisions.

Testing Objective

Improve customer response without introducing unnecessary campaign risk.

Business question

Which message, offer, timing or journey path produces a meaningful improvement in engagement or conversion while keeping unsubscribe, complaint and contact-frequency metrics within acceptable limits?

Decision standard

A test is only useful when the hypothesis, primary KPI, guardrails, sample split and decision rule are defined before launch. Results are read at the customer and journey level, not only by open or click rate.

Experiment Design

From hypothesis to rollout.

01

Define hypothesis

Example: a benefit-led subject line will increase qualified clicks without increasing opt-outs.

02

Select audience

Apply eligibility, consent, suppression and recent-contact rules before random assignment.

03

Create variants

Control and treatment use one intentional difference wherever possible.

04

QA assignment

Validate split logic, seed records, tracking, links, rendering and fallback content.

05

Run & observe

Monitor delivery, guardrails, sample balance and data quality during the test window.

06

Decide

Roll out, iterate, or retain control based on primary KPI, confidence and business impact.

Testing QA

Validate the experiment before measuring it.

Audience integrity

  • Mutually exclusive control/treatment groups
  • Randomization or deterministic split confirmed
  • Suppression and consent rules applied equally
  • No cross-cell contamination
  • Sample-size threshold documented

Creative & tracking

  • Only intended variable changes between cells
  • UTMs and conversion events mapped correctly
  • Links, personalization and fallback content tested
  • Rendering checked across priority devices
  • Seed list and proof approvals completed

Readout controls

  • Primary KPI defined before launch
  • Observation window is consistent
  • Guardrail metrics reviewed
  • Outliers and tracking gaps investigated
  • Winner documented with rollout recommendation
