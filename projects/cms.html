Project Overview

A central content library for faster, safer campaign production.

What Adobe Experience Manager is used for

Adobe Experience Manager provides a governed home for digital assets and structured content. Marketing teams can manage images, documents, renditions, metadata, localized variants and reusable content fragments in one place, while downstream websites and campaign systems retrieve approved content through APIs.

For lifecycle operations, the value is consistency: brand-approved content, current product information, locale-specific copy and expiration rules can be managed once and reused across email, SMS, push, in-app, landing pages and web experiences.

Reusable Content Blocks

Modular content that can change without rebuilding the whole message.

Instead of hard-coding every campaign from scratch, the delivery template can assemble approved modules. Each block has its own content ID, locale, owner, effective dates and fallback behavior.
